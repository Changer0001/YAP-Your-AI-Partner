# YAP Demo Bistro — Policies

_Last updated: 2025-08-15_

## Reservations & Cancellations
- We accept reservations for parties of 1–10.
- Deposits: $10 per person for parties of 6+ (refundable until 24 hours before).
- Cancellations: Please cancel at least 24 hours prior to avoid a $20 per person no-show fee.
- We hold tables for 10 minutes past your reservation time.

## Allergens & Dietary Needs
- We cook in a shared kitchen. Cross-contact may occur.
- Ask if you require a dedicated GF prep area; we'll do our best to accommodate.

## Service Animals
- Service animals are welcome. Non-service pets allowed on the patio only.

## Outside Food & Beverage
- Corkage: $20 per 750ml (max 2 bottles), waived with in-house bottle purchase.
- Outside dessert fee: $2.50 per person, max $20.

## Privacy
- If you book or order, we collect basic contact details to confirm your order/reservation.
- We do not sell personal data. Data is used to provide service, confirmations, and support.
- To request deletion of your data, email privacy@yap-demo-bistro.test.

## Payments
- We accept major credit cards and contactless payments. 18% gratuity may be added for 6+.
